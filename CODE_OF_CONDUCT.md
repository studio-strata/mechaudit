# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

MechAudit is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically rigorous community. Everyone
participating in project issues, reviews, discussions, events, and other project spaces is
expected to help create an environment where people can contribute without harassment or
intimidation.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and specific when discussing technical disagreements.
- Critique ideas, evidence, and code rather than people.
- Respect differing backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and communication styles.
- Give credit for ideas and work, and correct attribution mistakes promptly.
- Protect private information and unpublished research data.
- Accept reasonable maintainer direction intended to keep discussion safe and productive.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate
misgendering, doxxing, sustained disruption, retaliation, and publication of another person's
private information are not acceptable. Bad-faith use of project processes or security channels
is also prohibited.

## Scope

This policy applies in all project-managed spaces and whenever someone is publicly representing
the project. It also applies to private communication when that communication directly concerns
project participation.

## Reporting and enforcement

Conduct concerns may be sent privately through the repository's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/studio-strata/mechaudit/security/advisories/new) with
`[conduct]` at the start of the title.

If the form is unavailable, open a public issue that asks the maintainer to establish a private
channel, without including names, allegations, or sensitive details. Reports will be reviewed by
the maintainer identified in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md), subject to conflicts of interest.

Possible responses include a private warning, removal of content, temporary participation
limits, or permanent exclusion from project spaces. Enforcement decisions will consider impact,
context, prior behavior, and the safety of affected participants. Retaliation against a reporter
or participant in an investigation is prohibited.

## Attribution

This policy is project-specific and is informed by widely used open-source community standards,
including the Contributor Covenant. It is released with the rest of the project documentation
under the repository's license.
